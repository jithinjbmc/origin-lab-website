Research

Integrating organisms, genomes, landscapes and deep time

Focus areas

Research themes

Each theme connects organismal evidence with evolutionary questions and geographic context.

S

Recognising overlooked diversity

Systematics & Species Discovery

Comparative morphology, diagnostic characters, taxonomic revision and integrative species delimitation.

G

Reconstructing evolutionary history

Phylogenetics & Phylogenomics

Evolutionary relationships inferred using multilocus and genome-scale data, with explicit attention to uncertainty and concordance.

B

Connecting lineages and landscapes

Historical Biogeography

Testing how vicariance, dispersal, climate and geological history shaped present-day organismal distributions.

C

Evidence from specimens and deep time

Collections, Fossils & Imaging

Connecting modern biodiversity with museum specimens, fossil inclusions, microscopy and non-destructive imaging.

Questions

Questions that organise the work

Projects differ in organisms, geography and scale, but they share a commitment to explicit biological questions and traceable evidence.

How does geography structure evolutionary diversity?
How can overlooked species be recognised?
What can low-dispersal arthropods reveal about deep time?
How can genomic, morphological and geographic evidence be integrated?
How can taxonomy contribute to biodiversity conservation?
