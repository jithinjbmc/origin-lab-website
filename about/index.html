About ORiGIN Lab

Organismal Research in Genomics, Integrative Systematics and Natural History

Mission

Integrative organismal research

To investigate organismal diversity through natural history, taxonomy, morphology, molecular data, phylogenetics, genomics, biogeography and museum collections.

Scientific vision

Origins and diversification

We examine how biodiversity originates, diversifies, becomes geographically structured and persists through evolutionary and geological time.

Evidence

Integrative by design

Organisms, specimens, morphology, genes, genomes and landscapes are treated as complementary lines of evidence.

Public value

Documentation and conservation

Taxonomic and evolutionary research should support collections, open data, biodiversity documentation and conservation decisions.

Conceptual framework

The TerraEvo perspective

Earth history is part of evolutionary history.

The TerraEvo perspective links geological change, landscape evolution, geographic barriers, habitat structure, ecological persistence and dispersal limitation with evolutionary lineage formation. It is a research framework within ORiGIN Lab, not a separate public-facing laboratory.

Research principles

How we approach organismal biology

Specimens remain central

Field observations and natural-history collections provide essential evidence that cannot be replaced by sequence data alone.

Methods should converge

Taxonomy, morphology, genomics, dating and biogeography are strongest when their assumptions and uncertainties are evaluated together.

Geography has history

Modern distributions are interpreted in the context of barriers, climate, dispersal and deep-time geological change.

Outputs should remain useful

Research should improve names, diagnoses, collections, data availability and the evidence base for conservation.
